Mockup #742 — Business knowledge, one tree instead of six busy lists

Settings → Business knowledge, redesigned around issue #742: today's six flat facets (Review · Departments · Shelves · Families · Brands · Recognition) — each a paragraph plus a flat list, with nothing on screen saying where the words came from — become one line of provenance, one banner for the review queue (opened as a sheet, not a tab), ONE tree (Category → Shelf → Family, department shown as a chip), and two small cards for Brands and Recognition below it. Add / rename / delete on every row, one tap each, the same three affordances at every level. Example data only — two invented personas, nothing here is a real business.

1 · The home screen, collapsed requirement 1 + 2

The provenance line names where the words came from, with a one-tap "change" straight to the interview's cuisine pick and an ⓘ for the why. The old "358 suggestions" review tab is now a single banner — tap it and the existing review opens as a sheet (section 5), never the page's default view. Below that: a search box, then the tree, collapsed to one row per category with its shelf and family counts and its department as a small chip. Four category rows fit above the fold; the rest is an ordinary scroll, same as Today's own why-rows list.

What this replaces: the Departments, Shelves and Families facets (three flat lists all showing the same category → shelf → family relationship from three different angles, each with its own re-apply button and its own paragraph) fold into the one tree above. Brands and Recognition — which were never hierarchical to begin with — become two small summary cards under the tree, tapping through to their own detail. The Review facet, formerly the page's own busy default view, becomes a single banner that opens the unchanged review UI as a sheet. Nothing about the underlying data model changes — this is a render-and-interaction redesign over the same GET /item-model response, add/rename/remove reusing the existing POST /item-model/vocabulary, /subcategory/refile and /ingredients/:id doors; the build ticket is separate from this mockup.
