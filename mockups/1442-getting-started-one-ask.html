The flow, in one breath

  1. Four steps, the same for every business: Give · Check · Costs · Open. The bar is the whole progress story; no percentages, no minutes, no station map.
  2. One card at a time, in the agent's voice: one sentence, one button (or tiles as answers), Skip in grey. A done card folds to one line with its number.
  3. The route is invisible. "Where are your items?" decides it; a grocery gets price tags inside Check, a kitchen gets recipes and the chef's pass inside Check. The customer only sees more cards in that step.
  4. Nothing that is not a question is on the setup page. "What runs by itself" and the numbers appear after Go live, where they are the whole page.
  5. One "After this:" line is always under the current card, so the customer knows why he is being asked.
  6. Never asked: business type, cuisine, vendors, hours. They show as "I read it as X · change" lines after the first drop.
