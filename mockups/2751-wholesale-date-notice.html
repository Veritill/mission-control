Three questions for Kiran

  1. "Send what you have" only when something is here. The API refuses it while nothing has arrived for a line, but the page cannot tell today. Recommended: the notice line gains how many are here ("3 of 6 are here now", frame 1), and the button shows only then, worded with the number ("Send the 3 now, cancel the rest"). Otherwise the page shows all three and a tap can fail.
  2. Cancel asks once; Accept does not. Recommended: Accept and Send are one tap; Cancel shows "Cancel 4 bags of Toor dal? Keep it · Yes, cancel" in place (frame 2), since it cannot be undone from this page.
  3. The words "item" instead of "line". Recommended: the buyer sees "Cancel this item"; the API's "Cancel this line" stays the staff wording.
