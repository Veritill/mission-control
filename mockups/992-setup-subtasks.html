The one decision for Kiran — the strip's shape

A (recommended, phones 1–6): two rows — who, then what. Row 1 is one tile per vendor (its initials, three progress dots, "1 of 3"); row 2 is the selected vendor's three steps (Catalog · Check · Invoices). Why: 3 + 3 tiles fit a phone with the names readable (check 4); any vendor or any step is one tap away in either direction; the agent's "now" and the person's "you're here" can differ without confusion (phone 4). A door with one group (the POS door, phone 6) shows row 2 only.

B (alternative, last phone): one flat strip, the vendor name as a label over its three tiles. One row of state, but 9 tiles scroll off a phone, labels truncate, and "Harbor Dairy" is out of sight — the same hiding Kiran complained about on #601.

Where every number already comes from

GET /tenant/onboarding: vendors[] (per named vendor: catalog rows/created/published/working, invoices count/costable, skipped, askIds for Resume — issues #607/#810) draws the vendor row and its three dots; sections[] draws the main tiles; nextAsk (with fix.kind upload / verify / enter-cost and fix.batchId) names the agent's "now" tile and the work block; working drives the spinner chip; askHistory[] (with output, #908) is the History list. The POS door's Items/Sales dots read off the same tiers nextAsk already walks (items done → the sales ask appears). Nothing here needs a migration or a new route.
