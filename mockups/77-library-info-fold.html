📚 How the Product Library works

The Veritill Product Library holds universal product facts — the kind printed on any label or shelf tag: product name, brand, package size, category, barcode, and product descriptions. When people at any store verify these details, they join the library so every store's catalog fills in automatically.

The library contains product information only. Today it also learns how each supplier's paperwork reads, so a vendor your store has never trained can still arrive pre-understood.

You can manage your store's participation right here on Business settings.
