Option A — The ladder recommended

One page. The whole journey is a ladder of rungs (the list above, verbatim); vendors are sub-rungs with three dots each. Tap any rung and its work opens right under it — a drop zone, the wizard, the Toast fields. The sticky Next chip is the agent's one recommendation; the history is the ladder itself (done rungs keep their time and output). Left phone: the person tapped a vendor the agent did NOT point at — and can. Right phone: the ten-at-a-time wizard, inline.

Home · a vendor tapped, the drop zone inline

Same page · the verify wizard inline, then its output

For
  • The map and the work are the SAME surface — no layer to come back from.
  • Skipped looks skipped exactly where it is resumed.
  • Zero new routes; the history list and the section cards collapse into one list.
Against
  • Ten rungs on a phone — done rungs collapse to one line, the fold shows the chip, the bar and five rungs.
  • Rung pictures are emoji until eight icon files ship (vendor · ingredient · prep box · dish · register · team · money · go live).

Option B — The floor map most picture-first

The journey drawn as the place itself: the back door (vendors) → the walk-in (ingredients) → prep → the pass (menu) → costing → the till → the office → the OPEN sign; a grocery walks back door → shelves → price tags → count → till and scale → online → office → OPEN. Each station is a tile with its number and status ring. Below the map, the tapped station's board (one card per vendor); a step's work opens in a bottom sheet.
